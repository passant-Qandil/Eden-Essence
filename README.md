# Eden Essence
## Digital Marketing Graduation Project

### Project Overview
Eden Essence is a fragrance brand offering perfumes inspired by well-known luxury fragrance profiles, with a focus on quality, fragrance performance, customer trust, and accessible pricing.

This project aims to develop a data-informed digital marketing strategy to strengthen brand awareness, build customer trust, improve audience engagement, and support sales growth.

### Project Idea
The project focuses on understanding Eden Essence's target customers, evaluating its internal brand position, analyzing competitors and market conditions, and developing a strategic marketing foundation based on research and analysis.

### Work Plan

#### Step 1: Customer Analysis
- Analyze competitors' Facebook pages.
- Examine followers, engaged audiences, and publicly available customer reviews.
- Analyze 10 competitor accounts per team member.
- Record findings in a structured comparison table.
- Identify customer needs, interests, pain points, and engagement patterns based on available evidence.

#### Step 2: Internal Brand Analysis
- Review Eden Essence's products, pricing, and brand positioning.
- Evaluate the brand's visual identity and current digital presence.
- Review Facebook and Instagram content and audience engagement.
- Assess customer communication and available customer feedback.
- Identify internal strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.

#### Step 3: Competitive and PEST Analysis
- Analyze relevant competitors' products, positioning, content, and digital presence.
- Compare competitors with Eden Essence to identify competitive advantages and market gaps.
- Conduct a PEST analysis covering Political, Economic, Social, and Technological factors affecting the fragrance market.

#### Step 4: Strategic Marketing Planning
Based on the previous analyses:
- Develop a SWOT analysis.
- Set SMART marketing objectives.
- Create evidence-based buyer persona(s).
- Define Eden Essence's Unique Selling Proposition (USP).

### Step 5: Marketing Mix & Social Media Content Calendar

Based on the findings and strategic decisions from Steps 1–4:

#### 5.1 Marketing Mix (4Ps)
- Develop Eden Essence's marketing mix covering Product, Price, Place, and Promotion.
- Align product positioning and value proposition with the identified target audience and customer needs.
- Review pricing in relation to competitor pricing, perceived value, and the brand's accessible-premium positioning.
- Identify suitable sales and distribution channels based on the brand's current presence and available resources.
- Develop promotional recommendations that support brand awareness, customer trust, engagement, and sales objectives.
- Ensure all recommendations are supported by available research, with assumptions clearly identified.

#### 5.2 Social Media Content Calendar
- Create a social media content calendar from the implementation start date through November 30, 2026.
- Schedule content every other day.
- Select the most suitable platforms based on the target audience, brand presence, and strategic objectives.
- Define content pillars aligned with the brand's positioning, USP, buyer personas, and customer pain points.
- Specify the publishing date, platform, content pillar, post idea, content format, objective, CTA, and relevant KPI for each planned post.
- Balance educational, engagement-focused, product-focused, trust-building, and promotional content.
- Adapt content formats and messaging to the selected platforms.
- Monitor content performance and recommend adjustments based on available results.

**Deliverables:**
- Eden Essence Marketing Mix (4Ps).
- Social Media Content Calendar through November 30, 2026.
- Content strategy rationale and proposed performance measurement framework.
