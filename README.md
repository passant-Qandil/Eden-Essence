### Project Overview
This project focuses on understanding Eden Essence's target customers, analyzing competitors, and evaluating the brand's current digital presence to support informed marketing decisions.

### Step 1: Customer Analysis
- Analyze competitors' Facebook pages and relevant audience activity.
- Research followers, engaged audiences, and customer reviews where publicly available.
- Each team member analyzes 10 competitor accounts.
- Record findings in a structured comparison table.
- Identify customer needs, interests, pain points, and engagement patterns.

### Step 2: Internal Brand Analysis
- Review Eden Essence's products, pricing, and brand positioning.
- Evaluate its current Facebook and Instagram presence and content.
- Assess visible audience engagement and customer communication.
- Identify the brand's strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.
