
# Eden Essence
## Digital Marketing Graduation Project

A data-informed digital marketing strategy for Eden Essence, a fragrance brand offering perfumes inspired by well-known fragrances, with a focus on quality, fragrance performance, customer trust, and accessible pricing.

---

## 1. Project Overview

### Project Idea

The Eden Essence graduation project aims to develop a structured digital marketing strategy based on customer research, internal brand analysis, competitor analysis, and market evaluation.

The project focuses on strengthening brand awareness, building customer trust, improving audience engagement, helping customers discover suitable fragrance options, and supporting measurable purchase intent.

### Core Marketing Objectives

- Increase brand awareness and audience reach.
- Strengthen customer trust through accurate product information.
- Improve meaningful audience engagement.
- Help customers make informed fragrance choices.
- Encourage qualified inquiries and trackable purchase orders.
- Develop a content strategy aligned with the brand's positioning and marketing objectives.

## 3. Project Work Plan

### Step 1: Customer and Audience Analysis

- Analyze competitor Facebook pages and publicly available audience information.
- Examine followers, engaged audiences, and publicly available customer reviews.
- Analyze 10 competitor accounts per team member.
- Record findings in a structured comparison table.
- Identify customer needs, interests, pain points, and engagement patterns based on available evidence.

### Step 2: Internal Brand Analysis

- Review Eden Essence's products, pricing, and brand positioning.
- Evaluate the brand's visual identity and digital presence.
- Review Facebook and Instagram content and audience engagement.
- Assess customer communication and available customer feedback.
- Identify internal strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.

### Step 3: Competitive and PEST Analysis

- Analyze competitors' products, positioning, content, and digital presence.
- Compare competitors with Eden Essence to identify competitive advantages and market gaps.
- Conduct a PEST analysis covering Political, Economic, Social, and Technological factors affecting the fragrance market.

### Step 4: Strategic Marketing Planning

Based on the findings from the previous steps:

- Develop a SWOT analysis.
- Set SMART marketing objectives.
- Create evidence-based buyer personas.
- Define Eden Essence's Unique Selling Proposition (USP).

### Step 5: Marketing Mix and Social Media Content Strategy

#### 5.1 Marketing Mix (7Ps)

Develop a marketing mix aligned with Eden Essence's positioning, target audience, and business objectives.

- **Product:** Present accurate product information and fragrance descriptions based on verified details.
- **Price:** Communicate pricing and value without inventing discounts or unconfirmed offers.
- **Place:** Direct customers to approved product links and ordering channels.
- **Promotion:** Use appropriate content formats and promotional messaging to support awareness, trust, engagement, and purchase intent.
- **People:** Encourage customer questions and address audience needs and preferences.
- **Process:** Explain ordering, shipping, exchange, and return procedures based on verified policies.
- **Physical Evidence:** Use authentic product and packaging visuals and only publish genuine, verifiable reviews and claims.

#### 5.2 Content Strategy and Content Calendar

- Develop a content strategy aligned with the brand's USP, buyer personas, customer pain points, and marketing objectives.
- Create a social media content calendar for the campaign period.
- Define content pillars and the role of each content format.
- Specify the publishing date, platform, content pillar, idea, format, objective, CTA, and relevant KPI for each planned post.
- Balance educational, engagement-focused, product-focused, trust-building, and conversion-supporting content.
- Monitor available performance data and recommend improvements.

**Content Calendar Deliverables:**

- Content strategy and its supporting rationale.
- Social media content calendar.
- Content objectives and CTAs.
- Proposed performance measurement framework.

## 5. Marketing Performance KPIs

The following KPIs are aligned with the campaign's objectives and content strategy. They provide a framework for measuring performance rather than assuming results in advance.

### 5.1 Brand Awareness

**Objective:** Increase brand visibility and help new audiences discover Eden Essence.

- **Reach:** Number of unique accounts reached.
- **Impressions:** Total times content was displayed.
- **Reel Views:** Views generated by Reels.
- **Follower Growth:** Change in followers during the campaign.

### 5.2 Audience Engagement

**Objective:** Encourage meaningful interactions with the brand.

- **Engagement Rate:** Engagement relative to reach, using a consistent calculation method.
- **Saves and Shares:** Assess content usefulness and shareability.
- **Comments and Story Replies:** Measure audience interaction and interest.

### 5.3 Customer Consideration and Trust

**Objective:** Help customers explore fragrance options and make informed purchase decisions.

- **Profile Visits:** Measure interest in the brand.
- **Qualified Direct Messages:** Track relevant product and purchase inquiries.
- **Product Link Clicks:** Measure interest in specific products.
- **Customer Questions and Feedback:** Identify preferences and concerns.

### 5.4 Conversion and Purchase Intent

**Objective:** Support the transition from social media interest to qualified inquiries and trackable orders.

- **Qualified Inquiry Volume:** Number of relevant purchase inquiries.
- **Inquiry-to-Order Conversion Rate:** Percentage of qualified inquiries resulting in orders.
- **Completed Orders:** Number of confirmed, completed purchases that can be attributed to tracked sources.

### 5.5 Content Performance and Execution

**Objective:** Evaluate content formats and adherence to the publishing plan.

- **Reel Performance:** Reach, views, and engagement.
- **Carousel Performance:** Saves, shares, and engagement.
- **Static Post Performance:** Reach, comments, and engagement.
- **Publishing Adherence:** Percentage of planned content pieces published according to schedule.

### KPI-to-Content Alignment

- **Reels:** Support brand discovery and awareness.
- **Carousels:** Educate customers and support fragrance selection.
- **Static Posts:** Reinforce brand messaging, product information, and CTAs.
- **Trust-building content:** Address customer concerns using verified information.
- **Conversion-supporting content:** Encourage product exploration and qualified purchase inquiries.

### Measurement Guidelines

- Record baseline data before evaluating campaign performance.
- Establish numerical targets based on verified baseline data and available resources.
- Separate proposed targets from actual results.
- Use platform analytics and available order-tracking data to evaluate performance.
- Do not claim sales attribution or conversion results without reliable tracking.
